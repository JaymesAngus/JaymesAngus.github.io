## Interests

There are few topics I don't find fascinating, but I tend to focus on astrobiology,
computer simulation, and environmentalism. In recent years I've been particularly interested in the concept of emergence and in the capacity for
simulations to inform us about the material world.

When I'm not in front of a computer, I'm running, climbing, making music, or freezing in a tent.
